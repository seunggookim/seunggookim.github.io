[Mr. Augustin Bouquillard](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lcIdRWEAAAAJ&hl=fr) (Ingénieur Polytechnicien Program at École Polytechnique; doing two masters in CS and applied math in 4 years in [a Napoleon-like fancy uniform🧑🏻‍✈️](https://programmes.polytechnique.edu/en/ingenieur-polytechnicien-program/ingenieur-polytechnicien-program)) has successfully finished his internship at our group! During his internship, he worked on the Benchmark Suite of Musical Expectation (MEX; accepted for ISMIR-2026) and a series of behavioural validation experiments of the autogressive diffusion model that estimates Shannon entropy and surprisal directly from music audio data. We will keep working on this topic together in the future, and we are looking forward to publishing our results soon!
