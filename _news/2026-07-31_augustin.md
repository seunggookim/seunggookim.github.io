[Mr Augustin Bouquillard](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lcIdRWEAAAAJ&hl=fr), a student in the Ingénieur Polytechnicien Programme at École Polytechnique (pursuing two master's degrees in computer science and applied mathematics over four years, while wearing [a fancy Napoleonic-style uniform 🧑🏻‍✈️](https://programmes.polytechnique.edu/en/ingenieur-polytechnicien-program/ingenieur-polytechnicien-program)), has successfully completed his four-month internship in our group!

His internship was co-supervised by me, Dr Daniela Sammler (group leader), and Dr Stefan Lattner (Sony CSL Paris). During his internship, he worked on the **Benchmark Suite of Musical Expectation (MEX🌶️; accepted at ISMIR 2026)** and conducted a series of behavioural validation experiments on an autoregressive diffusion model that estimates Shannon entropy and surprisal directly from music audio.

We look forward to continuing our collaboration on this topic and publishing our results soon!
