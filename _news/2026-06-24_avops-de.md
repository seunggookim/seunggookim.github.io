The interdisciplinary workshop on Affective Voice Perception and Synthesis (AVoPS) was successfully held at the Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics (MPIEA) in Frankfurt am Main.

Dr Tirza Biron from the Weizmann Institute of Science, Prof. Juhan Nam, Prof. Jieun Song, and their students from KAIST, South Korea, joined us for a week of stimulating discussions and collaborations. I thank all participants and speakers.
