The interdisciplinary workshop on Affective Voice Perception and Synthesis (AVoPS) successfully took place at the MPIEA, Frankfurt am Main. Dr. Tirza Biron from Weizmann Institute of Science,Prof. Juhan Nam, Prof. Jieun Song, and their students from KAIST, South Korea, joined us for a week of exciting discussions and collaborations.
